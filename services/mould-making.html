Mould Making

Custom tooling solutions for precision manufacturing in Chennai

What is Mould Making?

Mould Making is the process of creating a mold (tool) used to produce plastic or resin parts. At VCAN 3D in Chennai, moulds are precision-engineered based on the customer's CAD design, enabling accurate and repeatable part production for vacuum casting and injection molding applications.

🔧 Types of Molds We Create:

  • Silicone mold (for Vacuum Casting)
  • Metal mold (for Injection Molding)
  • Prototype tooling for low-volume production
